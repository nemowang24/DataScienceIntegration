Morphology YOLO Project

I trained YOLOv8 to detect objects, used MediaPipe to identify face landmarks, register the landmarks with a 3D head mesh, use landmarks to calculate the position of headmesh. Then I calculate the position of identified 'fullledbox' object in the head mesh, use morphology to calculate the pose of ledbox. Then try to track the head movement using the ledbox.
